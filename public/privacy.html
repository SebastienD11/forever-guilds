Privacy and content removal

Forever Guilds is a public community archive. Guild names, realm names, character names, memories, reunion plans, and optional contact links submitted through the site are visible to everyone and may be indexed by search engines.

What is stored

We store only the fields submitted through the forms and their submission date. Cloudflare may process standard request and security data needed to host and protect the service. Turnstile checks submissions for automated abuse.

Retention

Community entries remain available while the archive operates or until a valid correction or removal request is processed. Deleted records may remain in Cloudflare D1 recovery history for the applicable Time Travel retention period.

Corrections, reports, and removal

Do not submit private or sensitive information. To report harassment, impersonation, spam, an unsafe link, or request correction or deletion, open a content report. Include the guild page URL and identify the specific content. Do not post additional private information in the report.

Contact links

External links are user-submitted and unverified. Review their destination before opening them. Forever Guilds is not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
